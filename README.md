## Files

| File | Use |
|:-----|:----|
| [`FDS-Card-Jacket_A4_3up_Color_v1.pdf`](FDS-Card-Jacket_A4_3up_Color_v1.pdf) | Color print; the warning text is red |
| [`FDS-Card-Jacket_A4_3up_Mono_v1.pdf`](FDS-Card-Jacket_A4_3up_Mono_v1.pdf) | Black-only print |

Both files hold the same layout: one A4 page with three jackets, a 100 mm ruler and a legend for the line styles.

## What you need

- An A4 printer and A4 paper
- Scissors or a craft knife
- Paper glue

## Print

1. Open the PDF and print it at actual size, 100%. Turn off "fit to page" and any scaling.
2. Measure the ruler at the bottom of the sheet. It must be exactly 100 mm. If it is not, the print was scaled; fix the print settings and print again.

## Assemble

The legend at the bottom of the sheet names the three marks.

| Mark | Meaning |
|:-----|:--------|
| Dotted line | Cut |
| Dashed line | Fold back |
| `GLUE` | Apply glue; the flap goes inside the jacket |

1. Cut each jacket out along its dotted outline.
2. Fold back along every dashed line.
3. Put glue on the flaps marked `GLUE`, fold them inside, and press the jacket closed until the glue sets.
4. Slide the disk card in.
